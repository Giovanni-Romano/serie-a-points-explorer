Method, coverage and limitations

Every club is truncated to its first M completed fixtures in chronological order. Clubs are ranked by recalculated points (3W + D), then goal difference, goals scored and name. Tied clubs have the same points, so tie-breaking does not affect the selected points value. These are reconstructed equal-games tables, not necessarily tables that existed on one calendar date or after a scheduled matchday.

Pre-1994 seasons are re-ranked under three-point scoring. This changes some positions compared with their original two-point tables. Multiplying old totals by 1.5 would be incorrect. Recalculation cannot adjust for changes in incentives or playing style.

Disciplinary deductions, title awards and official head-to-head tie-breaks are not reconstructed. Results may include awarded scores. Use this tool for on-field points comparisons; it cannot certify claims about sanction-adjusted official tables.

Only seasons earlier than the selected target season enter the comparison. A season is excluded if it lacks position n or M games for every club. League sizes and season lengths vary; use cohort filters. No imputation is used.
